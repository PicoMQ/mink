# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Mink.

The full contributor guide lives in the docs: [Contribute](https://minkdb.com/docs/contribute).

In short: small, focused PRs against `main`, keep the `mink/` / `query/` boundary intact, and [open an issue](https://github.com/PicoMQ/mink/issues) first for anything larger than a bug fix.
